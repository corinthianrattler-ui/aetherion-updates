# Aetherion Reforged v1.74.7

Living Roads, tournament realism, renown and companion progression update.

- Adds 45 persistent named road characters across Solara: hedge knights, squires, heralds, healers, merchants, farriers, ferrymen, scouts, couriers, caravan workers, shrine keepers and other travelers.
- Travelers move on real road routes over elapsed world time instead of teleporting. Weather and role affect pace; destinations and travel state persist in saves.
- News is physical: witnesses and travelers carry stories between places. Local knowledge and player renown spread only when a story actually reaches a settlement.
- Adds road encounters and a Roads & News ledger showing travelers, current journeys, local knowledge and worldwide renown.
- Adds recruitable Rowan Mire, Dame Elira Thorn, Ser Garran Wold and Pate Fen using their approved full-body portraits.
- Adds Iven Kestrel, Brother Caldus, Nessa Quill and Mara Fenlock as distinct tracked world characters with their approved art.
- Adds the House Dominus Tourney Lance to Valkorion's inventory with its approved artwork.
- Jousting now requires a living assigned horse, war saddle, bridle/tack, serviceable tourney lance, helmet and body armor.
- Adds Lucan Vale as a unique wandering companion squire. After at least 30 days of service, sufficient loyalty and renown, and restoration/control of Blood Keep Ruins, Valkorion may knight him into House Dominus. His name, role and portrait change to Ser Lucan Vale and he receives the black warhorse Sable.
- Preserves the v1.74.6 tournament calendar, house celebrations, House Dominus sky dial placement and Edwyn Rook portrait repair.
- Uses compact portrait atlases plus the dedicated Dominus lance art to keep the update smooth on mobile.
- No save reset is required.
